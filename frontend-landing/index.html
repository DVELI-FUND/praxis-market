How it works

Three steps from prediction to payout

STEP 01

Pick a market

Browse live prediction markets — crypto, politics, sports, finance, esports. Each market has a YES/NO binary outcome and a closing block height.

STEP 02

Take a position

Buy YES or NO shares with $PRX. The price reflects the market's current probability (e.g. YES at 68¢ means 68% implied odds). Your stake is locked until resolution.

STEP 03

Resolve & earn

When the market closes, bonded resolvers stake $PRX to propose the outcome. If your side wins, your shares pay out 100¢ each — plus a share of the resolver reward pool.

Features

Built for traders, resolvers, and builders

Self-custody wallet

Connect your Canopy wallet. Keys never leave your device. Full control over your $PRX and positions.

Binary AMM markets

Every market is a YES/NO outcome. Prices move as traders buy each side, reflecting real-time probability.

Bonded resolvers

Resolvers stake $PRX to propose outcomes. Correct proposals earn rewards; incorrect ones are disputed and forfeited.

Resolution center

Vote on disputed outcomes, file disputes, and participate in decentralized governance of market resolution.

Reward pools

Earn $PRX rewards from builder, community, investor, protocol, and resolver pools based on participation.

Watchlist & portfolio

Bookmark markets, track positions, and monitor net worth in real-time from your profile.

PRX token

Utility at every layer

Trading

Buy YES/NO shares with $PRX. Pay market fees (2%) that fund creator and resolver rewards.

Staking

Resolvers stake 500,000 $PRX to participate in market resolution. Higher stakes earn more weight.

Governance

Vote on disputes, propose market parameters, and shape protocol upgrades through on-chain governance.

Rewards

Earn from 5 reward pools: builder, community, investor, protocol, and resolver (tiered 1x/3x/7x by RRS).
